# NORTHPEAK (example brand)

Fictional outdoor drinkware brand used for the example ad. Replace with a real brand.

## Snapshot
- **What they sell:** insulated stainless steel bottles and tumblers
- **Marketplaces / channels:** Amazon US
- **Audience:** commuters, hikers, gym-goers 25–45 who care about build quality
- **Positioning in one line:** Keeps it cold longer, built to be dropped.

## Voice
- Tone: confident, plain-spoken, short sentences. No exclamation marks, no puns.
- Words we use: ice cold, all day, built, grip, leak-proof
- Words we avoid: premium, luxury, revolutionary, best

## Visual
- Font: Inter (900 display, 800 titles, 500 body)
- Logo: wordmark in all caps, 0.22em tracking, brand teal; white on dark
- Photography style: product on soft neutral gradient, no lifestyle clutter
- Icon style: 2.4 px rounded line icons, white on teal rounded squares

## Claims
- Allowed: 24h cold / 12h hot (internal lab test), 18/8 stainless, BPA-free, dishwasher safe, lifetime warranty
- Forbidden: "military grade", "unbreakable", any certification not listed above

## Do / Don't
- Do: lead with the temperature claim; use the dark trust bar on every gallery image
- Don't: use red; put text over the product; more than 4 callouts
