Northpeak makes insulated stainless steel bottles and tumblers for commuters, hikers and gym-goers who care about how a thing is built.
#1: Confident | Direct
We lead with the number: ice cold for 24 hours, hot for 12. We tested it, so we say it plainly and move on. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no puns. A Northpeak bottle is built to be dropped, tossed in a bag and used all day, and the copy sounds like it.
#2: Plain-spoken | Practical
We talk about what you can check: 18/8 stainless steel, a leak-proof lid, a base that fits a cup holder, a grip that stays dry. We never say premium, luxury, revolutionary or best. If a claim is not on the spec sheet, it is not in the ad.
