# Brand identity template

The copy is only as good as the identity it is written from. Two formats work.

## A. Two-trait format (preferred)

```
<One-sentence positioning: what the brand makes, for whom, and the promise.>
#1: <Dominant trait> | <Voice mode, e.g. Storytelling / Playful / Direct>
<A short paragraph in the brand's own voice that shows the trait: origin story, mindset, who it's for.>
#2: <Supporting trait> | <Voice mode, e.g. Confident / Warm / Wry>
<A short paragraph showing the second trait: how the product delivers, what the brand refuses to do.>
```

Example (Northpeak, the fictional example brand; also in `assets/examples/northpeak_brand_identity.md`):

```
Northpeak makes insulated stainless steel bottles and tumblers for commuters, hikers and gym-goers who care about how
a thing is built.
#1: Confident | Direct
We lead with the number: ice cold for 24 hours, hot for 12. We tested it, so we say it plainly and move on. Short
sentences. No exclamation marks, no puns. A Northpeak bottle is built to be dropped, tossed in a bag and used all day,
and the copy sounds like it.
#2: Plain-spoken | Practical
We talk about what you can check: 18/8 stainless steel, a leak-proof lid, a base that fits a cup holder, a grip that
stays dry. We never say premium, luxury, revolutionary or best. If a claim is not on the spec sheet, it is not in the ad.
```

## B. Single paragraph

```
Brand name: Northpeak. Confident and plain-spoken. Insulated steel bottles and tumblers for people who use them hard:
commuters, hikers, gym-goers. We lead with the temperature claim, keep sentences short, and never use exclamation
marks, puns or words like premium and luxury.
```

## Questions to ask when there is no identity yet

1. In one sentence, what do you make and for whom?
2. Three adjectives customers would use for the brand; one they never would.
3. Where is it made / who makes it / what's the origin story?
4. What do competitors say that you refuse to say?
5. Paste two ads or captions you were proud of.

Draft the two-trait format from the answers and the website, show it to the user, then write.
