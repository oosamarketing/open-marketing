# Worked examples

All three are written for Northpeak, the fictional example brand (insulated steel bottles). Every fact in them
comes from its brand notes; nothing here is a real company's ad.

## Meta — the temperature claim

Primary text:

```
🧊 Ice cold for 24 hours. Fill it in the morning, drink it cold at night.
...
Triple-wall vacuum insulation in 18/8 stainless steel. 32 oz, leak-proof lid, fits your cup holder.
...
Get the bottle that keeps up with your day.
```

Headline: `Ice cold for 24 hours.`
Description: `Leak-proof. Fits cup holders.`

## Meta — a use case (the commute)

```
☕ Coffee that is still hot when the train finally shows up.
...
The Northpeak Steel Bottle keeps drinks hot for 12 hours and cold for 24. The leak-proof lid means it rides in your
bag, not in your hand.
...
Shop the bottle built for the commute.
```

Headline: `Hot coffee, 12 hours later`
Description: `Built for all day`

Note how the hook alone (first block) reads complete within 125 characters, the body carries the product fact,
and the CTA closes with an imperative.
